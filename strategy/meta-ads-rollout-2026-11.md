## 8 · Compliance guardrails (every asset)

Brand Atlas `claims_check` **could not run**: no claim packs are activated for either neurora brand in Brand Atlas. Until they are, every asset gets a manual check against these rules:

1. **No effect claims.** Not "hilft beim Einschlafen", "fördert Schlaf", "beruhigt", "senkt Stress", "wirkt". Design intent is fine: „gebaut für die letzte halbe Stunde des Tages“ (C·03, HWG / EU cosmetics regulation).
2. **No biology in ads.** No "Thalamus", "limbisch", "parasympathisch" in Meta copy (C·01/C·02). The mechanism in ads is the **cue** (same scent, same time, one use), never a pathway.
3. **Meta personal-attributes policy:** never assert the viewer's condition („Du kannst nicht schlafen?“, „Leidest du unter …“). Use statements („Müde im Körper. Wach im Kopf.“) or „Für alle, die …“.
4. **Banned words:** Ritual (all forms), Wellness, Aromatherapie, Achtsamkeit, Self-Care, Energie, Vibes, Heilung, Reise, Schlafstörung, Einschlafprobleme, Stress, Angst.
5. **No exclamation marks, no emoji in the first line, Du-form, "neurora" lowercase, never the first word.**
6. **No faces** (D·07). If you want a creator/face test later, that is a brand-book exception for the owner to decide, not a default.
7. **No invented urgency.** The only deadlines used are the real gift end date and the real Christmas cut-off.
